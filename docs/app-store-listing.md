## Description (4000)
newnew is a calm news reader for iPhone and iPad. You choose the outlets; newnew reads their public feeds on your device, puts each story together once, and shows you how every outlet tells it.

YOUR OUTLETS, CHOSEN BY YOU
Nothing is chosen for you. Pick the outlets you want, category by category, from world news and business to sport, music, design and gaming. Add your own: any website's feed, a YouTube channel, or a Bluesky or Mastodon account.

EVERY STORY, TOLD ONCE
Headlines about the same event are grouped into one story and ranked by how widely it's covered and how fresh it is. See at a glance how many outlets have it and what's new since you last looked.

SEE HOW IT'S COVERED
Each story shows a coverage spectrum and every outlet's headline. With Apple Intelligence, tap to compare how outlets frame a story: written on your device, clearly labelled, never published.

ALWAYS THE PUBLISHER'S ARTICLE
newnew shows headlines and short extracts, and every story links to the publisher, opening their app when you have it.

TOP 10
Swipe through the ten most important stories right now, then the newest podcast episodes and videos from your outlets.

QUIET BY DESIGN
Follow or mute topics. Choose how the top of Today looks. Background refresh and breaking-news alerts are optional.

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. What you read stays on your device; your settings sync through your own iCloud.
