Privacy

NEWNEW: News, Grouped · Mutiny AS, Norway · updated 29 October 2026

In short

NEWNEW collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you choose and read stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

What stays on your devices

  • The outlets you choose, your own feeds and YouTube channels, and the topics you follow or mute.
  • Which stories you have read.
  • Summaries written by Apple Intelligence. They are made on your iPhone from headlines and short extracts; nothing is sent to us or to Apple's servers for this.

Deleting the app deletes all of it from that device.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your outlets, own feeds, categories, topics, which stories you have read (as short fingerprints of their links) and the look are kept in your own iCloud key-value storage, so NEWNEW is the same on your iPhone and iPad. Apple stores this for you under its own privacy policy; NEWNEW cannot see it. Summaries and the news itself are not synced: each device reads the feeds on its own.

What your iPhone fetches

  • The news: your iPhone reads the public RSS feeds of the outlets you choose, directly from each publisher, like any feed reader. Publishers see your IP address, as with any website. We never see which outlets you read.
  • The list of outlets to choose from, a small public file downloaded from newnew.news, which Cloudflare, Inc. hosts (older versions of the app download it from GitHub Pages). Cloudflare sees your IP address, as any website does.
  • Pictures come straight from the publishers' servers: only the picture each feed offers.
  • Your own feeds and YouTube channels are fetched by your iPhone directly from those sites, the same way.
  • Articles and videos you open are shown by the publisher, in its app if you have it, or in a browser inside NEWNEW. The publisher's own privacy policy applies there.

Breaking-news alerts

Alerts are off until you switch them on. If you switch on breaking news, your iPhone subscribes to NEWNEW's alerts through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), which delivers the notification. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy; NEWNEW receives no information about who subscribes. Alerts for topics you follow are decided on your iPhone.

Children

NEWNEW is a general news app and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: feedback@newnew.news.

For publishers

NEWNEW is an RSS reader: each iPhone reads the public RSS feeds its owner chose, shows headlines and the feed's own short description and picture, and links every story to the publisher. NEWNEW keeps no copy of your articles on any server. To have your outlet removed, or to change how it appears, write to publishers@newnew.news. We remove an outlet within 48 hours of a request; the change reaches every reader at their next refresh, without an app update.
