Privacy

newnew: News, Grouped · Mutiny AS, Norway · updated 29 October 2026

In short

newnew collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you choose and read stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The outlets you choose, your own feeds and YouTube channels, and the topics you follow or mute.
  • Which stories you have read.
  • Summaries written by Apple Intelligence. They are made on your iPhone from headlines and short extracts; nothing is sent to us or to Apple's servers for this.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What your iPhone fetches

  • The news: your iPhone reads the public RSS feeds of the outlets you choose, directly from each publisher, like any feed reader. Publishers see your IP address, as with any website. We never see which outlets you read.
  • The list of outlets to choose from, a small public file downloaded from GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub sees your IP address, as any website does.
  • Pictures come straight from the publishers' servers. When a feed has no picture, your iPhone asks the article's page for its preview picture.
  • Your own feeds and YouTube channels are fetched by your iPhone directly from those sites, the same way.
  • Articles and videos you open are shown by the publisher, in its app if you have it, or in a browser inside newnew. The publisher's own privacy policy applies there.

Breaking-news alerts

Alerts are off until you switch them on. If you switch on breaking news, your iPhone subscribes to newnew's alerts through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), which delivers the notification. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy; newnew receives no information about who subscribes. Alerts for topics you follow are decided on your iPhone.

Children

newnew is a general news app and is not directed at children.

Questions about privacy: feedback@newnew.news. Publishers who want their feed removed: publishers@newnew.news.
