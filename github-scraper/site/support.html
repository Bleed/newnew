(01) Questions
8Questions

Where does the news come from?

Your iPhone reads the public feeds of the outlets you choose, directly from each publisher. NEWNEW has no news server. You see headlines, a short extract and the feed’s own picture, and every story links to the publisher.

An outlet I want is missing.

Add it yourself in Settings › Outlets: paste a site or feed address (RSS or Atom), a Bluesky or Mastodon account, or a YouTube channel ID. Or write to us and we may add it to the list.

How often does NEWNEW update?

Each time you open it, and when you pull down to refresh. In the background iOS decides when, on Wi‑Fi by default. Change it in Settings › Background refresh.

Why is there no Apple Intelligence read on a story?

The read needs an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on. Some publishers don’t allow their text to be used with AI; a story covered only by them shows the headlines instead.

How do breaking alerts work?

They are off until you switch them on in Topics. An alert comes when many outlets report the same story at once, six a day at most. You can also be alerted about topics you follow.

Is NEWNEW the same on my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, if you’re signed in to iCloud: your outlets, own feeds, topics and what you’ve read follow you. Each device reads the news itself.

Does NEWNEW track me?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Read the privacy policy.

I’m a publisher.

Write to publishers@newnew.news. We remove an outlet within 48 hours of a request, or change how it appears.
