(01) Introduction

A calm way to read the news. NEWNEW is an RSS reader: it reads the outlets you choose, straight from their own feeds, and groups the headlines into stories, so you see who reports what, and how.

Apple Intelligence compares the coverage on your iPhone, never on a server. Every story links out to the publisher. No accounts, no tracking, no ads.
